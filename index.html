Can a large machine-learning catalyst model make a prediction that survives an actual electrochemical experiment?

Open Catalyst Project adsorption-energy calculations screen bimetallic surfaces and point to PdAu3, which is then synthesized and tested for oxygen reduction.

In brief: Rotating-disk experiments showed an improved ORR onset potential for polycrystalline PdAu3 nanoparticles compared with commercial polycrystalline Pt/C.

Can machine learning create a useful reference-electrode signal without putting a reference electrode in the battery?

A virtual reference electrode estimates negative-electrode potential from ordinary two-electrode signals and uses the estimate to detect lithium-plating risk.

In brief: Reported errors were RMSE 0.023 V and MAE 0.018 V; adaptive charging extended cycle life 8.25× versus CCCV under the tested low N/P-ratio condition.

Jiayi Yu et al.

11 August 2026
DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-76535-y

A two-stage ML workflow first classifies pH from DPV shape, then routes the signal to a pH-specific concentration model.

In brief: The pH classifier exceeded 85% accuracy across four environments from pH 3.6 to 8.0; pH-specific regressors estimated CEA over five orders of magnitude.

Wenjun He et al.
