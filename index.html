Can physics-guided machine learning make wearable electrochemical biosensor calibration more reliable?

A lightweight neural network adds a monotonicity constraint to electrochemical calibration, reducing error on a public wearable sweat-biosensor dataset while preserving physically plausible response trends.

In brief: The physics-guided model reported RMSE 0.9077 versus 1.8547 for linear regression and R2 = 0.7940 versus 0.1400, corresponding to about a 49% reduction in RMSE.

Can machine learning identify how axial ligands tune single-atom catalysts for CO2-to-formate conversion?

A small DFT dataset trains ML models that screen hundreds of metal-ligand combinations, while SHAP and electronic-structure calculations explain why the best axial ligand depends on the metal center.

In brief: An FNN trained on 81 DFT structures reached R2 = 0.94 and RMSE = 0.14 V on the independent test set; DFT rechecks of the top 17 candidates averaged 0.05 V prediction error.
